Terms of use

Updated 8 October 2026. Assist is a personal tool for its owner, not a service accepting public registrations. These pages explain that installation and do not grant access to the assistant.

Owner control

The owner controls the installation, connects accounts they are authorized to use, chooses the video playlist and configures external services. Calendar and YouTube are separate permissions. The YouTube integration is read-only; Calendar permission allows event changes as described on the home page.

Review the result

AI answers and summaries can contain errors or omit context. The owner should review proposed calendar changes and consult the original video or other source when accuracy matters. External API availability, account permissions and provider limits can interrupt processing; continuous availability is not promised.

Stopping use

The owner can stop the installation and revoke Google permissions at any time. Stopping access does not reverse previous calendar changes or remove saved records. The privacy notice explains data processing and the separate steps needed for deletion.

Google, YouTube, Telegram and configured AI services remain subject to their own terms. Support contact information for Assist is provided on the Google consent screen.
