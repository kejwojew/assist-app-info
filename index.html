Assist

A personal assistant for one owner, operated on the owner's computer and server and used through Telegram. Assist helps with calendar planning and prepares summaries and discussions of selected videos. It is not open for public registration.

Google Calendar

Assist reads calendars, event details and busy times to answer scheduling questions and prepare plans. With Calendar permission, it can create, update and delete events. Calendar edits proposed by the conversational assistant require the owner's confirmation. Separately configured background automation can maintain its designated calendars.

YouTube

A separate, read-only YouTube permission lets Assist verify ownership of a private playlist and read its video list. The owner selects the playlist. This integration does not add, remove or change videos or playlists. Available captions, audio transcripts and selected video frames support video summaries and discussion.

Processing and delivery

Assist stores working records and history on the owner's installation. Selected messages, calendar details and video material are processed by the configured AI provider; answers and selected content are delivered through Telegram. See the privacy notice for storage, sharing and deletion details.

Access and support

The owner authorizes each Google integration through Google's consent screen. This information site does not sign users in or request Google permissions. Support contact information is shown on the Google consent screen for Assist.
