Privacy notice

Updated 8 October 2026. Assist is a personal installation operated by and for one owner. This notice describes its Google Calendar and YouTube integrations and the assistant processing used with them.

Data accessed and its purpose

  • Google Calendar: calendar identifiers and names, event details such as titles, times and descriptions, and availability. These support scheduling, answers about the calendar, event proposals and authorized calendar changes.
  • YouTube: channel identifiers for ownership checks, the selected playlist's metadata and video entries, including video identifiers, titles, channels and order. Read-only access supplies the owner's video queue; it does not permit this integration to modify YouTube content.
  • Assistant records: messages, selected context, transcripts, video frames, generated replies and reports, delivery state and operational records. These support ongoing conversations, summaries and reliable delivery.
  • OAuth credentials: access and refresh tokens authenticate requests to Google. Refresh tokens are stored in the installation's local or server configuration; Assist does not ask for the owner's Google password.

Where processing happens

The owner's computer and server run Assist and store its database, configuration, cached media and any backups. Google receives API requests. The configured AI service, such as OpenAI or Anthropic, receives selected conversation context, calendar details or video material to produce answers and summaries. Telegram processes messages and media delivered through the bot. When video transcription is enabled, audio can also be processed on the configured transcription server.

These external services apply their own data-handling rules. This notice does not claim that their processing stays on the owner's devices or that they retain no copies. Assist uses the data described here to provide the owner's assistant functions.

Storage and retention

Assistant history, cached video data and reports do not currently have a fixed automatic deletion period. They remain in the installation until the owner removes them. Limiting the history supplied to an AI request does not delete the underlying records. Backups and copies held by external services require separate handling.

Stop access and delete stored data

The owner can review and revoke Assist's access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access to Google; it does not erase data already copied, undo calendar changes or delete Telegram messages.

To remove the installation's copies, the owner must stop the relevant processing and remove the stored credentials, database records, cached files and applicable backups. Assist does not currently provide a single automatic erase-all command. Messages and data retained by Telegram or an AI service must be managed through that service's controls. Google's account-access guidance explains the distinction between revocation and deleting previously shared data.

This information site and contact

These pages contain no sign-in form, analytics code or tracking scripts. The website host may process normal request logs. The support contact for the personal installation is shown on Assist's Google consent screen.
